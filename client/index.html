HOW TO PLAY

Controls:

  • A / D or Arrow Keys: Move paddle Left & Right
  • Spacebar / Left Click: Fire lasers / Launch ball / Continue
  • P / ESC: Open the pause menu (Continue, Replay, Main Menu, Volume)
  • M: Return to Main Menu immediately
  • 🔊 / 🔇 in the header: Quickly mute or restore sound. If sound is off, open the pause menu to turn it back up.

Modes & Features:

  • Campaign Mode: Progressive difficulty. Level 1 features an instant Domino chain reaction!
  • 3-Attempt System: You get 3 attempts per campaign. If you lose all 3 lives, your score is preserved and you can continue the current level twice (2 of 3 attempts remain after the first failure).
  • Survival Mode: Defend against descending waves of bricks. Do not allow any brick to cross the red Danger Line!
  • Neon Capsule Power-Ups: Distinct high-tech pills with clear glowing labels.
  • Combos: Maintain streaks without ball hitting paddle to trigger 2x and 3x score multipliers!
  • Cloud Progression: Log in to sync unlocked levels and compete on the global leaderboard.
